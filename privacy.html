Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Clipper is a personal tool I use to post short clips of my own videos to my own TikTok account. This page explains what it does with data.

What it accesses

Clipper accesses only my own TikTok account, and only through TikTok's official API. It uses that access to upload videos I choose. It does not read or collect data about anyone else.

Where data is kept

The access tokens TikTok gives Clipper are stored locally on my own PC. They are not sent to any other server.

Selling and sharing

Clipper sells nothing and shares nothing. No data is sold, rented, or given to anyone.

Disconnecting

I can disconnect Clipper from my TikTok account at any time, by revoking its access in TikTok's settings and deleting the stored tokens from my PC.

Changes

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions? Reach me through my GitHub profile.
